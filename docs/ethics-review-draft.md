## 1. Network Calls to Open Trivia DB
- **Stakeholders:** Learners (13–17), parents/teachers, developer, OpenTDB maintainers — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Privacy (*ACS Ethics 2.2(d)*), Network Security (*ACS Ethics 2.2(h)*).
- **Conflict:** Fetching trivia content requires network requests, exposing device IP addresses to OpenTDB servers.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Sent strictly category ID, difficulty level, question amount, and random session token over HTTPS. Rejected sending user identifiers, device IDs, or advertising IDs.
- **Decision & reason:** Restricting request parameters to bare content queries preserves learner anonymity.
- **Evidence:** `TriviaAPI.kt`, `TriviaRepositoryImpl.kt`, `AndroidManifest.xml` (only `INTERNET` permission), `NetworkMappingTest.kt`.

## 2. Choice of Question Categories
- **Stakeholders:** Learners (13–17), parents, teachers — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Minimise harm (*ACS Ethics 2.3.1*), age-appropriate content.
- **Conflict:** OpenTDB includes adult or sensitive trivia categories (e.g. Politics, Celebrities) unsuitable for secondary-school students.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Filtered and exposed strictly 9 age-appropriate domains via `CategoryFilter`. Rejected unrestricted category access.
- **Decision & reason:** Curating categories prevents accidental exposure to sensitive political or adult topics.
- **Evidence:** `CategoryFilter.kt`, `CategoryFilterTest.kt`, `TriviaRepositoryImpl.kt`.

## 3. Stars and Level Unlocking
- **Stakeholders:** Learners — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** User control (*ACS Conduct 1.2.2(d)*), motivation without manipulation.
- **Conflict:** Progression must encourage subject mastery without creating addictive or blocking mechanics.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Unlocking requires earning $\ge 1$ star ($\ge 50\%$). Cumulative best-stars record is kept so retrying a level never re-locks subsequent levels. Rejected zero-star progression gates and forced linear locks.
- **Decision & reason:** Mastery-based progression rewards effort while allowing learners to replay unlocked levels freely.
- **Evidence:** `LevelPolicy.kt`, `LevelPolicyTest.kt`, `ResultViewModel.kt`, `ResultViewModelTest.kt`.

## 4. Optional Question Timer
- **Stakeholders:** Learners (13–17) — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Minimise harm / reduce anxiety (*ACS Ethics 2.3.1*).
- **Conflict:** Timers add excitement but can trigger stress or cognitive overload for learners under pressure.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Default timer setting is `TimerMode.OFF`. Optional `RELAXED` (30s) and `CHALLENGE` (15s) modes available in Settings. Rejected mandatory enforced timers.
- **Decision & reason:** Opt-in timer configuration puts learners in control of their study pace.
- **Evidence:** `UserSettings.kt`, `TimerMode`, `QuizViewModel.kt`, `QuizEngineTest.kt`.

## 5. Result Screen Buttons & Non-Shaming Feedback
- **Stakeholders:** Learners, parents — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Honesty (*ACS Ethics 2.1(b)*), user control (*ACS Conduct 1.2.2(d)*), non-manipulative design.
- **Conflict:** Informing learners of quiz scores without inducing shame or forcing continuous play.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** "Back to map" and "Next level" buttons have equal visual weight (`OutlinedButton`, `weight(1f)`). Neutral guidance sentences shown by star rating ("Not passed yet – that's part of learning"), with TalkBack `liveRegion = Polite`. Rejected auto-advancing next level, punitive sounds/animations, and negative words ("fail", "wrong").
- **Decision & reason:** Equal weighting and supportive guidance allow learners to pause naturally or replay comfortably.
- **Evidence:** `ResultScreen.kt`, `ResultScreenTest.kt`, `strings.xml` (`result_guidance_*`).

## 6. Daily Goal and "Done for Today" Cue
- **Stakeholders:** Learners, parents — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Minimise harm (*ACS Ethics 2.3.1*), cognitive fatigue prevention.
- **Conflict:** Encouraging daily study without fostering screen addiction.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Gentle "Done for today" cue card appears when daily goal is reached ("It is a good time to take a break!"). Does not block further play or penalize stopping. Rejected streak counters, aggressive pop-ups, or streak loss threats.
- **Decision & reason:** Gentle stopping cues respect learner well-being and encourage healthy study breaks.
- **Evidence:** `DailyGoalTracker.kt`, `DailyGoalTrackerTest.kt`, `HomeScreen.kt`, `ResultScreen.kt`.

## 7. Statistics Without Streaks
- **Stakeholders:** Learners — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Honesty (*ACS Ethics 2.1(b)*), non-manipulative motivation.
- **Conflict:** Visualizing progress without using coercive streak mechanics.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Track learning days active this week ("Learning days this week: N") and 7-day activity bar charts. Rejected consecutive day streak counters and streak repair microtransactions.
- **Decision & reason:** Active day counts celebrate progress without punishing missed days.
- **Evidence:** `StatsRepositoryImpl.kt`, `StatsViewModel.kt`, `StatsScreen.kt`, `StatsScreenTest.kt`.

## 8. Review Flashcards (Spaced Repetition)
- **Stakeholders:** Learners — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** User control (*ACS Conduct 1.2.2(d)*), effective learning.
- **Conflict:** Reinforcing memory without overwhelming the learner with infinite queues.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Leitner 5-box spaced repetition (`LeitnerScheduler`). Failed answers enter Box 1, correct answers advance boxes. Equal-weight "I knew it" / "Not yet" review buttons. Shows "All caught up!" stopping cue when queue is empty.
- **Decision & reason:** Structured intervals support long-term memory while maintaining reasonable daily review bounds.
- **Evidence:** `LeitnerScheduler.kt`, `LeitnerSchedulerTest.kt`, `ReviewRepositoryImpl.kt`, `ReviewViewModel.kt`, `ReviewScreen.kt`.

## 9. Data Retention Period on Privacy Screen
- **Stakeholders:** Learners (13–17), parents — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Privacy / Data Minimization (*ACS Ethics 2.2(d)*), clarity (*ACCC v Google [2021] FCA 367*).
- **Conflict:** Balancing answer history persistence with privacy protection for minors.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Placed answer retention control directly on Privacy screen under "How long we keep it". Default retention is 30 days (`DAYS_30`), with options for 90 days or "Until I delete it". Purges expired records immediately upon change. Rejected hiding retention in separate settings menus or defaulting to forever.
- **Decision & reason:** Co-locating retention explanations with controls ensures informed consent and minimal data storage.
- **Evidence:** `RetentionPolicy.kt`, `UserSettings.kt`, `PrivacyScreen.kt`, `PrivacyViewModel.kt`, `PrivacyViewModelTest.kt`.

## 10. Complete Export and Delete My Data
- **Stakeholders:** Learners, parents — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** User control (*ACS Conduct 1.2.2(d)*), privacy (*ACS Ethics 2.2(d)*).
- **Conflict:** Providing complete data portability and deletion without complex permissions or data loss risks.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** SAF file export (`CreateDocument` picker) exporting a complete JSON file including scores, answers, review cards, settings, and reported questions (`UserDataExport`). One-tap "Delete All Data" with equal-weight neutral dialog ("Keep my data" vs "Delete everything").
- **Decision & reason:** SAF file export uses zero permissions and avoids Binder limits; neutral delete buttons prevent deceptive dark patterns.
- **Evidence:** `ExportWriter.kt`, `UserDataExport.kt`, `ExportSerializerTest.kt`, `PrivacyRepositoryImpl.kt`, `PrivacyScreen.kt`, `PrivacyScreenTest.kt`.

## 11. Cloud Backup Rules
- **Stakeholders:** Learners, parents — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Privacy (*ACS Ethics 2.2(d)*), data containment.
- **Conflict:** Preventing sensitive answer records from syncing automatically to commercial cloud providers.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Configured `data_extraction_rules.xml` and `backup_rules.xml` to exclude Room SQLite database files from automatic Google Cloud Backups.
- **Decision & reason:** Keeps learner quiz records strictly contained on-device.
- **Evidence:** `data_extraction_rules.xml`, `backup_rules.xml`, `AndroidManifest.xml`.

## 12. Flag a Question & Undo Report
- **Stakeholders:** Learners, OpenTDB community — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** User control (*ACS Conduct 1.2.2(d)*), undoing as easy as doing.
- **Conflict:** Allowing learners to hide bad/inappropriate questions permanently without permanently locking them out if done by mistake.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Flagging a question immediately hides it across quizzes and review queues (`FlaggedQuestionEntity`). Privacy screen displays reported question count with an "Undo" restore option ("Show them again").
- **Decision & reason:** Reversible flagging gives learners full sovereignty over their content filters.
- **Evidence:** `FlaggedQuestionDao.kt`, `QuizViewModel.kt`, `PrivacyRepositoryImpl.kt`, `PrivacyScreen.kt`, `PrivacyScreenTest.kt`.

## 13. Sound, Animations & Contrast Verification
- **Stakeholders:** Learners (including those with visual or sensory sensitivities) — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Accessibility (*ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*), contrast guarantees.
- **Conflict:** Rich media and animations enhance engagement but can cause distraction, motion sickness, or readability failures.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Optional sound effects via `SoundPool`. "Reduce motion" toggle in Settings that overrides Compose transitions with instant `snap()`. Dynamic color is disabled to preserve WCAG 2.1 AA contrast guarantees ($\ge 4.5:1$ text contrast verified programmatically).
- **Decision & reason:** Tested contrast ratios and motion overrides guarantee accessibility across diverse user needs.
- **Evidence:** `ContrastChecker.kt`, `ThemeContrastTest.kt`, `Theme.kt`, `AndroidSoundPlayer.kt`, `SettingsScreen.kt`.

## 14. No Notifications
- **Stakeholders:** Learners (13–17) — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Minimise harm / prevent nagging (*ACS Ethics 2.3.1*).
- **Conflict:** Push notifications can boost daily engagement but rely on urgency and attention-hijacking.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** The app contains zero notification code, scheduling workers, or push receivers.
- **Decision & reason:** Respecting learner attention means never sending unsolicited re-engagement prompts.
- **Evidence:** `AndroidManifest.xml` (no notification permissions/receivers), codebase inspection.

## 15. Plain-Language Privacy Notice
- **Stakeholders:** Secondary-school learners (13–17), parents — *ACS Conduct 1.2.1(a)*.
- **Values at stake:** Honesty & Transparency (*ACS Ethics 2.1(b)*).
- **Conflict:** Traditional privacy policies are written in dense legalese that minors cannot understand.
- **Option chosen (and options rejected):** Wrote all disclosures on the Privacy screen in short, plain-language sentences without technical jargon (no "telemetry", "HTTP", or "database"). Explains clearly what is stored, what is never collected, and what OpenTDB sees.
- **Decision & reason:** Teen-friendly plain language ensures meaningful consent and data safety understanding.
- **Evidence:** `strings.xml` (`privacy_*`), `PrivacyScreen.kt`, `PrivacyScreenTest.kt`.

## Dark-Pattern Checklist (Gray et al., 2018)

| Dark Pattern Category | Description | How QuizQuest Avoids It | Code / Design Evidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nagging** | Repeatedly interrupting user flow to request actions (e.g. notifications, review popups). | Zero push notifications, zero rate-us popups, zero intrusive prompts. | `AndroidManifest.xml` contains no notification permissions or broadcast receivers. |
| **Obstruction** | Making data export or account deletion artificially difficult or hidden. | One-tap SAF file export (`CreateDocument`) and one-tap "Delete All Data" on Privacy screen. | `PrivacyScreen.kt`, `ExportWriter.kt`, `PrivacyRepositoryImpl.kt`. |
| **Sneaking** | Hiding costs, tracking, or unexpected data flows behind user's back. | Plain-language disclosures of all network requests (OpenTDB HTTPS) and zero tracking SDKs. | `strings.xml` (`privacy_opentdb_desc`), `AndroidManifest.xml` (`INTERNET` permission only). |
| **Interface Interference** | Using visual hierarchy, colors, or pre-selected options to manipulate choices. | Equal visual weight (`OutlinedButton`, `weight(1f)`) for all binary choices (e.g. "Keep my data" / "Delete everything"; "Back to map" / "Next level"). | `ResultScreen.kt`, `PrivacyScreen.kt`, `ReviewScreen.kt`. |
| **Forced Action** | Mandating account creation, social logins, or permissions to use core features. | Zero user accounts, zero sign-in screens, zero location/contacts permissions. | `MainActivity.kt`, `appModules` in `Modules.kt` operates entirely locally. |
